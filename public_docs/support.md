## Common questions

**Why won't apps block?**
Bittersweet blocks apps using Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) API, which needs
your permission. Go to **Settings → Blocklist** in the app and make sure Screen Time
access is granted, then confirm you've selected at least one app to block.

**Why is my focus rating wrong?**
Ratings are estimated on your device from motion data. If Motion & Fitness access is
off, or the session was short, there may not be enough signal to estimate. You can
always set the stars yourself on the session summary, or switch the tag to
"self-rated" so motion never sets the rating.

**Where did my fruits go?**
Fruits are spent when you unlock a blocked app, buy items in the Fruit Store, or mark
days off to protect a streak. Your full history is in the Journal.

**I reinstalled the app — is my data gone?**
No, as long as you were signed in. Sign-in is restored automatically on reinstall and
your sessions, tags, goals, and fruits are pulled back from the cloud.

**How do I cancel Premium?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the iOS **Settings** app → tap your name →
**Subscriptions** → **Bittersweet** → Cancel. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the
period ends prevents the next renewal.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app, go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This permanently removes
your data from our servers.
