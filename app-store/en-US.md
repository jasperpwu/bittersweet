## Description (4000)
Your attention is worth something. Bittersweet makes that literal.

Start a focus session and the apps you've chosen go dark. Finish it and you earn fruits — a currency you can spend. Every distraction has a price, paid out of focus you actually earned. Not willpower. Just a price tag.

FOCUS THAT PAYS
• Timed or open-ended sessions, tagged however you organize your life
• Distracting apps blocked at the system level through Apple's Screen Time — not a reminder you can swipe away
• Lock Screen Live Activity, Dynamic Island, and home screen widgets to start in one tap
• Spend fruits to unlock a blocked app for a few minutes when you genuinely need it, no awkwardness trying to solve a puzzle when you need to show your phone to your friends.

RATINGS THAT HAPPEN ON THEIR OWN
• Your phone's motion estimates how focused each session was, and scales the fruits you earn
• Stationary tags reward staying put, active tags like running reward moving, self-rated tags leave the stars to you
• Every estimate is computed on your device. Motion data never leaves it, and you can turn it off.

GOALS, STREAKS, BADGES
• Daily, weekly, monthly, or cumulative targets on any tag
• Rest days built in, plus Off-Markers to protect a streak on days you plan to be away
• Streaks tracking
• Conclude a goal and it becomes a badge summarizing your time spent on something, easily memorable

A WEEKLY FOCUS COACH
• Every finished week gets a report: a focus score broken into consistency, quality, and volume
• Grounded reads on your peak hours, your strongest day, and how the week compared to your average
• On Apple Intelligence devices, an on-device model writes it. Your sessions are never sent anywhere, privacy is first.

GROVE — FOCUS WITH FRIENDS
• See friends' real focus sessions. No comments, no infinite scroll, just positive energy.
• Weekly/Monthly leaderboard, and exactly where you stand on it
• Challenges: commit to a tag target together and double the fruits you earned with it — on your own record, or bound to the whole group
• Send fruit gifts to the people you're rooting for
• Heartbeat: if you go quiet for days or change block list settings, your inner circle notices, this is your accountability support

ALSO INSIDE
• A journal timeline of every session, with manual entry for the ones you forgot
• TODOs that turn straight into focus sessions
• Apple Health import — finished workouts become sessions automatically
• Fruit Store: Accelerate Cards, Off-Markers, and rewards you define yourself
• English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese

FREE AND PREMIUM
Free tier gives you 3 tags and 1 active goal. Premium unlocks unlimited tags and goals, apple health integration, and more, billed monthly or yearly. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Bittersweet needs Screen Time permission to block apps. Grove and sync are optional — the app is fully functional on its own.
